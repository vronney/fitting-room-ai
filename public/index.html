Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in your phone's browser. To keep it one tap away, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How accurate is it?

It shows how the piece looks and drapes on your body. It can't promise the tag size fits — check the size before you buy.

What counts as a look?

Every image we make for you, including Try again. Cancelling, errors and blocked requests never use one.

Do my looks expire?

Pack looks never expire. Your free looks don't either.
