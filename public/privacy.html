Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

What we do with your photos

Your photo and the photos of the clothes are sent to our server only for the seconds it takes to make your look, then passed to Google's Gemini image model to generate it. We don't store them on our servers. We never build a face or body profile, and we don't use your photos to train anything.

Your generated looks are kept on your phone only, for 24 hours, so you can compare them. Save the ones you want; the rest clear themselves.

Who else sees them

Google processes the images under its paid-API terms, which don't use your content to improve its models. We don't sell or share your photos with anyone else.

What we keep

Nothing personal today: no account, no email, no cookies. Your phone keeps three things locally: the 18+ tick for two hours, your in-progress photo and items for two hours (so a camera reload doesn't lose them), and your looks for 24 hours. If we add paid packs, your receipt lives with our payment processor (Stripe), not with us.

Your rights

If an image of you was made without your permission, tell us and we remove it within 48 hours. Questions: hello@offtherack.app.
