Safety

Your photo stays yours.

A try-on app handles the most personal photo you own. These rules are built in, not bolted on.

  • 18+ only. You confirm it's you and that you're 18+ before your first look.
  • Clothes on, always. Requests to undress or sexualize anyone are blocked, and you're barred.
  • Never stored. Your photos never sit on our servers, and we never build a face or body profile.
  • Report and remove. Anyone can ask us to take down an image of them, and we remove it within 48 hours.
