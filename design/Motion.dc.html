Safety

Your photo stays yours.

A try-on app handles the most personal photo you own. These rules are built in, not bolted on.

  • 18+ only. You confirm it's you, or that you have permission, before every upload.
  • Clothes on, always. Requests to undress or sexualize anyone are blocked and the account is closed.
  • Deleted in 24 hours. We never build a face or body profile.
  • Report and remove. Anyone can ask us to take down an image of them, and we act within 48 hours.
